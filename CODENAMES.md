## 🚀 Release Showcase Details

### 🌌 v0.1.6 — Event Horizon
- **Tag**: `v0.1.6` • **Build**: `8` • **Channel**: `stable`
- **Release Date**: November 22, 2026
- **Codename Lore**: *The boundary marking the point of no return around a black hole, where velocity reaches the speed of light.*
- **Key Breakthroughs**:
  - **Fast Prefix-Matching Engine**: Spotlight search matches prefixes (`startsWith(query)`) instantly for near-zero latency app launches.
  - **Cross-Theme Top Hits**: Added the quick-launch row across all 5 app menu & drawer themes (`Spotlight`, `Elegant`, `Grid`, `Terminal`, `Minimal`).
  - **Intelligent Fallback**: Automatic word-boundary matching fallback when direct app prefix matches are absent.

---

### 🌑 v0.1.5 — Dark Matter
- **Tag**: `v0.1.5` • **Build**: `7` • **Channel**: `stable`
- **Release Date**: November 01, 2026
- **Codename Lore**: *The unseeable substance comprising the vast majority of the universe's mass, holding galaxies together.*
- **Key Breakthroughs**:
  - **Self-Healing Favorites Engine**: Resilient matching that resolves apps across package updates even when launcher Activity class names change.
  - **Vintage Cassette Deck (`RetroMusicWidget`)**: Fully animated spools, VU meters, and tactile transport buttons.
  - **Debounced Tap Gestures**: Single-tap play/pause, double-tap skip, triple-tap previous across all music widget variants.
  - **Wallpaper Personalization Hub**: Dedicated `WallpaperSettingsPage` with real-time blur, tint, and timing controls.

---

### ☁️ v0.1.4 — Nebula
- **Tag**: `v0.1.4` • **Build**: `6` • **Channel**: `stable`
- **Release Date**: November 01, 2026
- **Codename Lore**: *Interstellar clouds of dust and gas where new stars and planetary systems are born.*
- **Key Breakthroughs**:
  - **On-Demand Google Fonts**: Removed static font binaries from APK assets, streaming 9+ Google Fonts on-demand with local disk caching.
  - **Decoupled Hardware Layer**: Extracted hardware, sensor, and telemetry logic into a clean `system.*` architecture.
  - **Deterministic Home Navigation**: Replaced scattered boolean flags with a unified `HomeOverlay` state machine.

---

### 🌀 v0.1.3 — Singularity
- **Tag**: `v0.1.3` • **Build**: `5` • **Channel**: `stable`
- **Release Date**: October 03, 2026
- **Codename Lore**: *A point of infinite density at the gravitational heart of a black hole.*
- **Key Breakthroughs**:
  - **Automated Build Pipeline (`build.sh`)**: Unified POSIX release engineering pipeline reading metadata directly from `RELEASE`.
  - **Automated In-App Updater**: Periodic background polling of GitHub Releases with custom `UpdatePromptDialog` and streaming APK installation.
  - **Cryptographic Verification**: Automatic SHA-256 checksum generation into `dist/SHA256SUMS`.

---

### ⚛️ v0.1.2 — Quantum
